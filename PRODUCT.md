# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and self-learners who want to quickly understand any topic. They arrive with a question in mind and want structured, clear study notes — not a chat conversation. Context: studying for exams, exploring a new subject, or refreshing knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Instantly generate well-structured study notes from any topic. The product succeeds when a student gets a clear, actionable explanation with a visual diagram in under 10 seconds, and feels confident they understand the topic.

## Brand Personality

Clean, calm, focused. The interface should feel like a quiet library — minimal distraction, clear hierarchy, space to think. Not loud, not busy, not trying to impress.

## Anti-references

- Claude/ChatGPT-style chat UI (no chat bubbles, no conversation paradigm)
- SaaS dashboard with sidebar (not a tool-with-chrome, keep content-first)
- Generic AI wrapper (no gradient-everything, no shiny AI aesthetic)

## Design Principles

1. Content is the product — typography and spacing serve readability above all
2. One task per screen — search, generate, read. No competing CTAs.
3. Calm confidence — the interface should disappear into the task

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Prefer system fonts for body text. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Ensure sufficient contrast for extended reading sessions.
